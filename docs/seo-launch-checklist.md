# SEO Launch Checklist

Use this checklist after the production deployment. Record evidence, dates, and account owners beside each completed item.

## Deployment and crawlability

- [ ] Deploy the approved build and `curl` every canonical URL, all legacy URLs, and an unknown URL; record status codes and redirect locations.
- [ ] Verify the HTTPS apex host is canonical and the `www` host redirects to it.
- [ ] Verify Google Search Console ownership for the production property.
- [ ] Verify Bing Webmaster Tools ownership for the production property.
- [ ] Submit `https://msmonsterglobal.com/sitemap.xml` in both webmaster tools.
- [ ] Request indexing for the English and Bahasa Melayu home, IT maintenance, commercial aroma, aroma diffuser, and custom fragrance routes.

## Business identity and citations

- [ ] Claim or verify an eligible Google Business Profile using the confirmed legal business details.
- [ ] Reconcile Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, LinkedIn, and legitimate business citations so the name, address, phone, URL, and category data agree.
- [ ] Verify and document the AMECO relationship before publishing any ownership, affiliation, distribution, or other cross-brand claim.

## Live sharing and measurement

- [ ] Test the live WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X cards; refresh each platform cache if stale metadata or imagery appears.
- [ ] Record baseline branded-query and qualified-query visibility, impressions, clicks, indexed-page counts, and conversions without promising ranking positions.
